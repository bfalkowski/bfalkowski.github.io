About

I build AI features for enterprise applications at Appian, including generative AI workflows for requirements capture, application planning, test generation, and developer assistance.

Most of my work is about making AI behavior testable and ready for production. That means instrumentation, evals, error analysis, and quality gates. Recent work includes distributed tracing with OpenTelemetry, model behavior analysis, trace-based cost optimization, and repeatable LLM-as-a-judge workflows.

I have an M.S. in Data Science from Rochester Institute of Technology, where my graduate work focused on machine learning, NLP, and evaluation reliability.
