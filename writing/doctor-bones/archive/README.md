# Doctor Bones archive

Made-up things the young man in [Doctor Bones](../../doctor-bones.html) collected about
Dale before he built him. The story links here from "everything the real Dale had ever put
on the internet", from a short "The archive" section under the scene, and from Dale's
"Built from" line in the scene's inspector.

Rules for the items (from the author's continuity notes): everything is made up; no real
accounts, addresses, venues, schools or employers; Dayton only at city level; Dale was
seven in 1974 (born about 1967); Doctor Bones the dog never appears in any item.

Every page has `noindex` and a "FICTION" bar at the top that links to the story and back
to the manifest. The platforms are made up ("Professional network", "daybook", "keepsake"),
not real ones. The pages come from the author's archive folder (v0.5); the copies here add
the bar and `noindex`, use "keepsake" for the photo site, caption the church photo "The
church" (no real venue), and hide the cover-photo name on the social profile, which sat
behind the profile picture.
