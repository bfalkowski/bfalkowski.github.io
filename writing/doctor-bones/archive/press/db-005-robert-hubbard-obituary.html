Robert James Hubbard, 1936–2004

Robert James Hubbard, 68, of Dayton, died Friday, November 19, at a Dayton hospital after a brief illness.

Born in Dayton on June 14, 1936, he was the son of the late Walter and Irene Hubbard. He worked for many years as a tool-and-die maker before retiring in 1998. Bob was a longtime member of his bowling league and rarely missed a Saturday morning breakfast with friends.

He is survived by his wife of 46 years, Evelyn; two sons, Dale Hubbard and his wife, Marjorie, of Dayton, and Michael Hubbard and his wife, Karen, of Kettering; and four grandchildren. He is also survived by a sister, Patricia Miller, of Xenia.

Bob was known for his steady humor, his willingness to lend a hand, and his habit of fixing things that other people had already given up on. His family will remember him most for the ordinary evenings spent around the kitchen table.

Services

Visitation will be held Tuesday, November 23, from 5 to 8 p.m. at the family funeral home. A funeral service will take place Wednesday at 10 a.m., followed by burial at a Dayton-area cemetery.

In lieu of flowers, the family suggests donations to a local hospice organization.
