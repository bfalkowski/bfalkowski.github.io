About the agency

Hubbard Insurance Services is an independent insurance office serving individuals and small businesses in the Dayton area. The agency works with clients on personal auto, homeowners and small-business coverage, with an emphasis on explaining options in plain language.

Owner Dale Hubbard has worked in the insurance business since 1989 and established the independent agency in 1998. The office describes its approach as practical and personal: review the coverage, answer questions, and help customers understand what they are buying.
